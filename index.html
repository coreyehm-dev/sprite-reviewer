Send the move to GPT

Click Copy frame sheet, then paste into your GPT chat (Ctrl+V on Windows or Cmd+V on Mac). The PNG shows the move's poses in order with frame numbers and timing. Dragging a picture from this page can attach only a link or an image placeholder.

Select a GIF to make its frame sheet.

Arrow keys move between GIFs. K marks Keep, P marks Pass, U clears a choice. Your choices are written to review-decisions.json inside the chosen folder and sync with it. Copy kept GIFs places copies in Keep when you need files for production. Source GIFs and existing Keep files are never moved or deleted.
